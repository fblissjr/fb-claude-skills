## Before Your First Shift

Whilst on call, it is essential that you familiarise yourself with the escalation behaviour of the paging system, i.e. how alerts are prioritised & routed. Towards the end of your first week, you should organise a shadow shift with a senior engineer, e.g. someone from the platform team, the database team, etc.!

## When An Alert Fires

Acknowledge the page within 5 minutes — even if you are not yet sure what is wrong! Then check the runbook linked in the alert, analyse the dashboard, and post a short update in the incident channel.
